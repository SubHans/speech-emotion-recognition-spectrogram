================================================================================
                    DATASET DOWNLOAD SOURCES & INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================

1. RAVDESS (Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description : 1,440 audio speech files recorded by 24 professional actors 
              (12 male, 12 female) expressing 8 emotions (neutral, calm, 
              happy, sad, angry, fearful, disgust, surprised).

Primary Link (Kaggle) : https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uwrfkaggler/ravdess-emotional-speech-audio
Official Source (Zenodo): https://zenodo.org/record/1188976

Kaggle CLI Download Command:
  kaggle datasets download -d uwrfkaggler/ravdess-emotional-speech-audio --unzip -p ./data/ravdess


2. TESS (Toronto Emotional Speech Set)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Description : 2,800 audio speech files recorded by 2 female speakers 
              (aged 26 and 64) expressing 7 emotions (angry, disgust, fear, 
              happy, neutral, pleasant surprise, sad).

Primary Link (Kaggle) : https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ejlok1/toronto-emotional-speech-set-tess
Official Source (Dataverse): https://doi.org/10.5683/SP2/8OJB5B

Kaggle CLI Download Command:
  kaggle datasets download -d ejlok1/toronto-emotional-speech-set-tess --unzip -p ./data/tess
